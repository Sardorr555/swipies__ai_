FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know
about integration

How does the AI agent connect to our ERP or database?

Integration uses standard REST/OData APIs, webhooks, or a secure local gateway with strict read/write scope isolation.

Can the agent make unauthorized changes to records?

No. Any write action (creating an invoice, reservation) follows rigorous schema validation and can require explicit approval.

Can we connect private on-premise databases?

Yes, on-premise deployments can communicate through dedicated site-to-site VPN tunnels without opening public ports.

How long does a standard integration take?

Standard CRM and ERP connector setups take 3 to 7 business days, including data mapping and workflow verification.
