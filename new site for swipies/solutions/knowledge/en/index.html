BEFORE YOU START

Questions that matter.

Contact the Swipies team
Is this part of the Swipies platform?

Yes. The document assistant runs on the shared platform. Use your existing account or register, connect a knowledge base and configure your agent.

Can I upload PDFs and Word documents?

The platform works with documents including PDF and DOCX, as well as text materials. Recognition quality depends on the file's structure and content. Check the processing results before launch.

Will every answer include a source reference?

Source references depend on the agent settings, retrieved passages and display method. Configure and test this with your own materials; an answer should not be attributed to a document when no source is found.

What if the knowledge base has no answer?

Set a rule to say when information is missing and to clarify the question. The assistant should not invent provisions that are absent from the materials. Check important decisions against the original.

Who can see the documents?

Use the workspace access controls and settings. Before providing access, check which knowledge is connected to the agent and how it is published.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on your plan, usage and integrations. View the terms in Swipies or ask the team. This page does not take payments.
