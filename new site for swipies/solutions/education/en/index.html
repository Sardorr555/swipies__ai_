FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers about the
education AI assistant

Where does the agent get course information?

You upload your school brochures, price lists, syllabi, and FAQs into the Swipies platform. The assistant relies exclusively on those documents.

Can it speak Uzbek and Russian fluently?

Yes, the assistant natively supports Uzbek (Latin & Cyrillic), Russian, and English, matching each student’s chosen language automatically.

What happens when a question requires a human?

When a student asks an edge-case or administrative question, the agent politely informs them and escalates the conversation to your staff with full context.

How long does implementation take?

Standard setup takes 1–2 business days: uploading materials, reviewing response behaviors, and connecting your Telegram bot or website widget.
