FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Key questions about
HR agent implementation

How does the assistant pre-screen applicants?

The assistant asks key qualifying questions (years of experience, core tech stack, location, salary expectations) and packages answers into a structured candidate card.

Is company policy and employee data secure?

Yes. Your knowledge base and conversation logs reside in isolated storage with encryption at rest and in transit. Data is never shared or used to train general models.

Can the bot initiate PTO or certificate requests?

Yes, the bot collects request details (dates, leave type) and dispatches them to HR or your HRM system via API.

Does it support bilingual Uzbek and Russian hiring?

Yes, the assistant natively supports Uzbek (Latin/Cyrillic) and Russian, providing an exceptional experience for local talent in Central Asia.
