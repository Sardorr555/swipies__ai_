BEFORE YOU GO LIVE

The essentials, explained.

Contact the Swipies team

Is this part of the shared Swipies platform?

Yes. AI support runs on the shared platform. You can use your existing account, knowledge base and the tools available on your plan.

Where can I connect AI support?

Telegram, Instagram Direct, your website, your app and other channels. The connection method and available actions depend on the platform and your access. We can configure a custom integration where needed.

Where do the answers come from?

From the materials and sources you connect. You keep them up to date and set the behaviour rules, including what to do when an answer is missing.

What if the agent does not know the answer?

You can define clarifying questions and escalation to your team. The agent should not invent terms or order statuses. Test these situations before launch.

Will it check an order or create a helpdesk ticket by itself?

That requires the appropriate tools and integrations. Without them, the agent can explain instructions and collect context in the conversation, but should not confirm unknown information or promise that a ticket has been created.

How much does it cost to connect?

The cost depends on your plan, usage and required integrations. Check the terms in the platform or ask our team. This page does not take a separate payment.
