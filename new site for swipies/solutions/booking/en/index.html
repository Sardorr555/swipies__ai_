BEFORE YOU GO LIVE

What you need to know.

Contact the Swipies team
Is this a separate service or part of Swipies?

This is a solution on the shared platform. Use your existing account or register, add service knowledge and configure your agent.

Will it create an appointment in my calendar by itself?

That requires a configured integration that checks availability and has permission to create appointments. Without it, the agent can clarify preferences and prepare a request, but should not promise a confirmed booking.

Can appointments be rescheduled or cancelled?

These actions are connected separately according to system capabilities and your business rules. Define who may change an appointment and how changes are confirmed.

What if the time is already taken?

A working workflow should check current availability before confirmation and handle conflicts. Test the integration rules and behaviour before launch.

Where can I connect the agent?

Telegram, Instagram Direct, your website and other channels. Capabilities depend on the platform, access and configured tools. Custom integrations are discussed individually.

How much does it cost to connect?

The cost depends on your platform plan, usage and integrations. Check the terms in Swipies or ask the team. This page does not take payment or create a real appointment.
