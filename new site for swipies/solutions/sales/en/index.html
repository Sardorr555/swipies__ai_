BEFORE YOU START

The essentials, explained.

Have another question?
Contact the Swipies team

Where can I connect my AI seller?

Telegram, Instagram Direct, your website, your app and other communication channels. The capabilities and connection method depend on the platform and your access. We help you choose an existing connector or configure a custom integration.

Is this a separate service or part of Swipies?

This solution runs on the shared Swipies platform. Sign in with your existing account or register, add a knowledge base and configure your agent.

How does the agent learn about my products and prices?

From the materials you connect: catalogues, descriptions, price lists and business rules. Keep these up to date. Reading information from a CRM or another system requires the relevant connection.

Could it invent a discount or give a wrong answer?

AI can make mistakes. Set clear rules, ground answers in your materials, define when to involve a manager and test workflows before launch. Do not let the agent promise terms it is not allowed to change.

Do I need a developer?

You can configure the knowledge base and agent workflow in the platform. Connecting channels may require help from our team or your specialist. Custom integrations are discussed separately.

Will it place an order and save it to my CRM?

That depends on the tools and integrations you configure. You can start with consultations and collecting enquiries in the conversation. Order creation, CRM writes and other actions need their own setup and testing.

How much does it cost to connect?

The cost depends on your platform plan, usage and required integrations. View the terms in Swipies or ask our team. This page does not take payments or require a purchase.
